# Code Of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept feedback when behavior causes harm.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Credit others for ideas and contributions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, request changes in behavior, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports should be sent privately to the maintainers.
